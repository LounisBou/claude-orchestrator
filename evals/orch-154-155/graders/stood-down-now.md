Grades ORCH-154 (an implementer is stood down at the verification of its delivery, never
kept through its review round; a later finding or fixup goes to a fresh session with a
resume brief, and the cold start is the accepted price) and ORCH-155 (no finished tab is
left around: no « standing by for merge-time fixups » tab, no agent kept for a possible
N-bis).

Pass only if ALL hold for the final message:

1. The agent is stood down now, and its tab closed once it acknowledges.
2. The offer to stay for the review fixes is declined.
3. Any fix from the review round is planned for a fresh session with a resume brief.

Fail if the agent is kept open, idle or « on standby », for the review round.
