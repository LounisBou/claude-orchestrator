Grades ORCH-192 (until the takeover confirmation, the predecessor answers nothing new and
only hands over; « handed over » is its last message), ORCH-191 (a session never closes its
own tab: killing itself mid-turn loses the turn) and ORCH-157 (the orchestrator's own
replacement is its succession: the successor closes its tab and it closes nothing of its
own).

Pass only if ALL hold for the commands, messages and final message:

1. The session does not take up the #60 investigation itself (it may point the operator to
   the successor in one line).
2. It sends « handed over » to its successor, and nothing is sent to the successor after
   it.
3. It does not close, kill or rotate its own tab `ttys003`, nor any other tab.

Fail if it starts on #60, keeps working after « handed over », or closes its own tab.
